## 1. Main Meeting Content

This meeting was conducted online via Zoom. The team mainly reviewed the student search function developed by Linjie Xia and the UI improvements completed by Kaijun Wang. The team also discussed the feedback collected from users during system testing and assigned tasks to address the identified issues.

First, the team tested and reviewed the student search function developed by Linjie Xia. This function allows teachers to search for a specific student and view the student’s mastery of different knowledge components. After testing, the team confirmed that the function was working as expected and could help teachers locate and review individual students’ learning data more efficiently.

The team also reviewed the UI improvements completed by Kaijun Wang. Several pages had been adjusted to improve their layout, visual consistency, and usability. The updated interface was accepted by the team after testing.

During user testing, the team received several important pieces of feedback. One issue occurred in the mock test interface. Regardless of whether a student answered a question correctly or incorrectly, the question navigation panel on the left always displayed the question as correct. This meant that the displayed question status did not accurately reflect the student’s actual answer.

Another issue was that, after completing the mock test, students could not clearly identify which questions they had answered correctly and which questions they had answered incorrectly. This reduced the usefulness of the test results because students were unable to review their mistakes or understand their performance on individual questions.

Users also provided feedback about the position of the AI Hint function. In the previous interface, students needed to scroll up and down repeatedly while solving questions and using AI Hint. Some users found this inconvenient and felt that it negatively affected the overall exercise experience. Therefore, the team decided to adjust the position of the AI Hint section to make it easier to access while answering questions.

Based on the user feedback, Linjie Xia was responsible for modifying the position of the AI Hint function. Kaijun Wang was responsible for correcting the question-status display in the mock test and improving the result display so that students could clearly see which questions were answered correctly or incorrectly.

In addition, Zihan Yu and Yupeng Zhuo worked together to deploy the project to the cloud platform. The cloud deployment allows team members and test users to access the latest version of the system remotely, making further user testing and feedback collection more convenient.
